What Is CI Payout Actually For?
💸
Income Replacement
When you can't work, CI pays out cash to cover your daily living expenses — so you don't have to drain your savings.
🏥
What Your Medical Card Doesn't Cover
Private nursing, nutritional supplements, traditional medicine, and non-hospitalisation treatments — your medical card won't pay for these. CI does.
🏠
Loan Repayments
Your mortgage and car loan don't stop because you're sick. CI ensures you don't lose your assets during recovery.
👨‍👩‍👧
Keeping Your Family Afloat
School fees, household expenses, supporting your parents — your responsibilities don't pause. CI keeps your family protected while you recover.
✈️
Access to Better Treatment
Money gives you options. CI lets you choose a better hospital, a specialist, or even seek treatment abroad if needed.
🧘
Focus on Recovery, Not Bills
Financial stress slows down healing. CI gives you the peace of mind to recover fully without worrying about money.
